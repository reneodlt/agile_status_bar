Support

Agile Bar shows the current Octopus Agile electricity price in the macOS menu bar. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are handled on GitHub: github.com/reneodlt/agile_status_bar/issues. You do not need to be a developer to open one — plain English is fine, and every issue is read.

Common questions

I installed it but nothing opened

That is expected. Agile Bar has no window and no Dock icon — it lives entirely in the menu bar. Look at the right-hand end of the menu bar at the top of your screen: you should see a price in pence, such as 24.3p, with a small coloured dot beside it. Click it to open the full interface.

On a Mac with a notch, a crowded menu bar can hide items. Quit some other menu bar apps, or use a menu bar manager, to make room.

The prices are not right for where I live

Agile prices differ by electricity grid region, and the app defaults to region C (London). Click the price, then the gear icon, and either pick your region from the list or type your postcode and press Find.

Why can it only see a few hours ahead?

Octopus publishes the next day's Agile prices in the late afternoon, usually around 16:00. Before then, less than 24 hours is genuinely known. Rather than implying a full day of data it does not have, the app tells you how far ahead it can actually see — "next 8h 30m", for instance.

Do I need an Octopus account or an API key?

No. Agile Bar reads Octopus Energy's public, unauthenticated tariff feed. There is nothing to sign in to, no API key to paste, and no meter hardware required. It works whether or not you are an Octopus customer.

The price does not match my bill

A few reasons this is normal. The app shows the unit rate only — your bill also includes a daily standing charge. Prices can be shown with or without VAT, which is a toggle in Settings. And the rate must be for your own grid region to be comparable.

Agile Bar is for information only. Always check your Octopus account for what you are actually billed.

It is showing a negative price

That is real, not a bug. Agile prices genuinely go below zero when the grid has more generation than demand — you are paid to use electricity. The chart keeps a true zero baseline so those slots read as what they are.

How do I start it automatically, or quit it?

Click the price, then the gear icon, and tick Open at login. To quit, click the price and choose Quit at the bottom of the panel.

What does the colour mean?

Prices are coloured against the rest of the visible day: blue for cheaper than usual, grey for typical, red for dearer. The scale re-bases itself daily, so it stays meaningful as the wholesale market moves. Colour is never the only signal — every use is paired with a text label.
